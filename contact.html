Start with the regulatory problem.

You do not need a technology brief. A useful first discussion establishes the regulatory outcome, the decision that needs to be made and the evidence already available.

Useful starting questions

What harm or risk needs to be prevented or reduced?

Who is regulated, and what behaviour or compliance outcome is required?

What does the regulator need to do differently?

What decision about operating model, performance or technology is approaching?
