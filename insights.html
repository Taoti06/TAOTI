Insights

Questions that expose whether the enterprise is joined up.

Strategy

What choice are we actually making?

Strategy should establish outcomes, trade-offs and direction. If everything remains a priority, the organisation has not made a strategic choice.

Portfolio

What should we stop?

Portfolio stewardship is not the accumulation of approved projects. It is the continuing allocation of scarce money and capacity to the highest-value work.

Customer & product

Are we funding a project or stewarding a service?

Products and services need continuing decisions about customer need, performance, investment, technical sustainability, improvement and retirement.

Operating model

Where do the hand-offs fail?

Cross-functional problems often sit in the interfaces: unclear decisions, fragmented accountability, duplicated capability and dependencies nobody owns.

Benefits

Did the investment produce the value we approved?

Benefits should inform continuing investment and course correction, not become a reporting exercise after delivery.

Technology

What enterprise outcome does the technology enable?

Architecture, platforms, data and digital investment should be traceable to customer, service and organisational capability—not treated as a separate technology agenda.
