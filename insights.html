Insights

Questions to resolve before technology becomes the answer.

Operating model

A regulator does not need a CRM strategy. It needs to know how it intends to regulate.

Define harms, behaviours, interventions, decisions and information before selecting the platform.

Behaviour

What behaviour is the intervention meant to change?

Regulatory activity is useful only when there is a defensible line between intervention, behavioural effect and outcome.

Engagement

Communication can be a regulatory intervention.

Guidance, disclosure, education, consultation and direct engagement can change behaviour and generate regulatory intelligence.

Technology

When a technology problem is an operating-model problem.

Fragmented accountability, unclear decisions and inconsistent process often surface as a demand for a new platform.

Performance

Measure regulatory effect, not simply regulatory activity.

Ask whether behaviour, risk or harm changed — not only whether inspections, contacts or milestones were completed.

Assurance

Trace the technology back to the harm.

If a major design decision cannot be traced through information, operating model and regulatory response to the problem being addressed, examine it.
