Insights

Questions that should be answered before the solution is chosen.

Regulatory design

What problem is the regulator trying to change?

Begin with harm, risk, regulatory purpose and the behaviour required. Activity is not a substitute for a defined regulatory outcome.

Behaviour

What affects compliance?

Obligations matter, but so do knowledge, capability, incentives, barriers and the regulated party's response to the regulator.

Engagement

Communication is part of the regulatory toolkit.

Guidance, education, disclosure, consultation and engagement can influence behaviour, support compliance and generate intelligence.

Operating model

Do not turn an operating-model problem into a software procurement.

Unclear decisions, fragmented accountability and inconsistent processes do not disappear when a new platform is introduced.

Performance

Measure regulatory effect, not only regulatory activity.

Inspections, contacts and cases show what the regulator did. Performance should also show what changed in behaviour, compliance, risk or harm.

Technology

Trace technology requirements back to regulatory purpose.

A workflow, data field, integration or platform decision should have a clear reason in the regulatory operating model.
