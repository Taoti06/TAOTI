Insights

Questions to resolve before technology becomes the answer.

Operating model

A regulator does not need a CRM strategy. It needs to know how it intends to regulate.

Define harms and risks, regulated entities and duty holders, required behaviours, regulatory responses, decisions and information before selecting the platform.

Behaviour

What regulated-entity or duty-holder behaviour is the regulatory response meant to change?

Regulatory activity is useful only when there is a defensible line between the regulatory response, regulated-entity behaviour and outcome.

Engagement

Communication, engagement and behaviour change are regulatory tools.

Guidance, disclosure, education, consultation and direct engagement can build understanding, influence behaviour, support compliance and generate regulatory intelligence.

Technology

When a technology problem is an operating-model problem.

Fragmented accountability, unclear decisions and inconsistent process often surface as a demand for a new platform.

Performance

Measure regulatory effect, not simply regulatory activity.

Ask whether regulated-entity behaviour changed, compliance improved, or risk or harm reduced — not only whether inspections, contacts or milestones were completed.

Assurance

Trace the technology back to the harm or risk.

If a major design decision cannot be traced through information, operating model and regulatory response to the harm or risk being addressed, examine it.
