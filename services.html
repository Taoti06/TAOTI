Services

Regulatory design, performance and technology.

Each service addresses a defined decision in the regulatory lifecycle. The work can stand alone or follow the same chain from problem definition through operating model, technology and performance.

01

Regulatory Diagnostic

What problem requires regulatory attention?

For a Commissioner or executive considering regulatory reform, organisational change or investment.

We examine the harm and risk, regulatory purpose, regulated parties and duty holders, obligations, behaviour, evidence, current regulatory responses, performance, operating arrangements, information and technology.

Output: a defined regulatory problem, evidence base, key causes and constraints, decisions required and recommended next steps.

02

Regulatory Operating Model

How should the regulator work?

We design the regulatory response and the organisational capability needed to deliver it.

The work can cover harm and risk, regulated-party and duty-holder analysis, behaviour, communications and engagement, regulatory tools, regulatory journeys, capabilities, processes, decision rights, governance, workforce, partnerships, information, data and performance measures.

Output: a target regulatory operating model, performance model and transition priorities.

03

Regulatory Technology Design

What must the technology enable?

For regulators considering licensing, case management, complaints, compliance, intelligence, analytics, digital services, CRM, AI or platform modernisation.

We derive the requirement from the regulatory operating model: users, regulatory journeys, decisions, workflows, evidence, records, data, integrations, reporting, analytics and controls.

Output: regulatory, information and technology requirements suitable for architecture, investment planning, market engagement and procurement.

04

Regulatory Technology Advocate

Who represents the regulator when technology decisions are made?

This service is for regulators whose technology is delivered or governed elsewhere in government.

We work on the regulator's side through investment proposals, architecture, shared-platform decisions, procurement, vendor discussions, product design and delivery. We maintain traceability between the regulatory requirement and the technology being proposed.

Output: independent regulator-side advice, decision support and continuity of regulatory requirements through the technology process.

05

Regulatory Performance & Transformation Review

Is regulatory effort producing the intended effect?

For an existing regulatory model, reform program or technology investment.

We test the chain from regulatory purpose to performance: whether measures distinguish activity from effect; whether compliance, intelligence and performance information support risk-based decisions; whether the operating model supports the regulatory response; and whether enabling technology supports the operating model.

We then examine the evidence of changes in behaviour, compliance, risk and harm.

Output: findings on regulatory effectiveness and enabling capability, together with decisions and corrective actions.
