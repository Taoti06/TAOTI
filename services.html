Services

Regulatory design and technology.

Defined pieces of work for regulators making decisions about reform, operating models, technology and delivery.

01

Regulatory Diagnostic

What problem are we actually trying to solve?

For a Commissioner or executive considering reform, investment or technology.

We examine the harm, behaviour, evidence, existing interventions, regulatory performance, operating arrangements, information, data and technology.

Output: a concise diagnosis of the problem, causes, capability gaps, risks, decisions required and recommended next steps.

02

Regulatory Operating Model

How should this regulation work in practice?

Design the regulatory response and the organisation required to deliver it.

Harm and risk model. Behavioural analysis. Intervention model. Engagement. Regulatory journeys. Capabilities. Processes. Decision rights. Governance. Workforce. Information. Data. Measures.

Output: a target regulatory operating model and transition plan.

03

Regulatory Technology Design

What does the technology actually need to do?

For regulators considering CRM, licensing, case management, complaints, compliance, intelligence, analytics, digital services, AI or platform modernisation.

We start from the regulatory model and define users, capabilities, workflows, information, data, decision points, integrations and technology requirements.

Output: a technology requirement grounded in the regulatory operating model and ready to inform architecture, investment and procurement.

04

Regulatory Technology Advocate

Independent technology advice for the regulator.

For regulators that depend on departmental or shared government technology functions to deliver their systems.

We represent the regulatory requirement through business case, investment, architecture, shared-platform decisions, procurement, vendor discussions, product design and delivery.

The job is not to sell a platform. It is to make sure the regulator gets technology that supports the way it needs to regulate.

05

Regulatory Transformation Review

Is the investment improving regulation?

For a program already underway.

We trace the program backwards: Technology → Information → Operating Model → Regulatory Response → Behaviour → Harm.

Where that chain breaks, we examine why and what needs to change.

Output: executive findings, decisions, corrective actions and assurance about the connection between investment and regulatory performance.
