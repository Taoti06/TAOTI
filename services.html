Work

Strategy, integration and executable change.

The work begins with the enterprise decision that needs to be made. Engagements can stand alone or follow the same chain from diagnosis and direction through investment, delivery and benefits.

01

Enterprise Diagnostic

What is the problem we actually need to solve?

For executives facing reform, investment, organisational change or a program that is not producing the intended outcome.

We examine strategy, customer and stakeholder evidence, service performance, operating model, capability, investment, governance, technology and data, dependencies, risk and delivery constraints.

Output: a clear problem definition, evidence base, enterprise implications, choices and recommended next steps.

02

Strategy & Operating Model

What future are we choosing, and how must the organisation work?

We translate outcomes into enterprise capabilities, operating arrangements and a practical path to implementation.

The work can cover enterprise strategy, customer and service outcomes, capability maps, target operating models, roles and decision rights, governance, organisational interfaces, performance and transition sequencing.

Output: strategic choices, target state, operating model, governance and sequenced roadmap.

03

Portfolio & Investment

What should we invest in, and in what order?

We establish portfolio visibility and decision disciplines across services, products, programs and enterprise initiatives.

Prioritisation considers strategic alignment, customer and public value, affordability, organisational capacity, dependencies, architecture, risk, delivery confidence and benefits.

Output: investment logic, prioritisation criteria, portfolio governance, sequencing and decision-ready recommendations.

04

Technology, Data & Product

What must digital capability enable?

For organisations modernising platforms, data, digital services, customer products or enterprise technology.

We connect customer and service outcomes, operating-model requirements and product lifecycle with architecture, data, integration, cyber, technology lifecycle, investment and delivery.

Output: target capability and direction, requirements, investment choices, roadmap and governance that maintain line of sight to the enterprise outcome.

05

Transformation & Benefits

How do we turn the decision into sustained value?

For major transformation, reform or investment requiring cross-functional delivery and executive oversight.

We establish governance, sequencing, dependencies, delivery assurance, change and adoption, performance measures and benefits ownership. Where delivery crosses organisational boundaries, we integrate the specialist parties around the outcome.

Output: executable transformation plan, governance and assurance, measurable benefits and stronger internal capability.
