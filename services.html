Work

We work where policy, regulation, operations and technology meet.

Calm Agency can work at the front end—before a reform, procurement or investment is locked in—or come into work already under way to test the design, reset the program or get delivery back on track.

01

Strategy & Regulatory Design

What problem are you trying to solve?

We help departments and regulators turn legislation, policy, duties and government priorities into a practical strategy.

That means being precise about the harm or outcome, duty holders, risk, desired behaviour, regulatory posture, interventions, measures and accountabilities. We test whether the proposed response is proportionate to the problem and whether it can actually be delivered.

Typical work: enterprise strategy, regulatory strategy, duty models, regulatory frameworks, outcome and performance measures, reform design and implementation planning.

02

Operating Model & Process

How will the work actually happen?

We turn strategy into the machinery of the organisation: roles, decision rights, workflows, governance, information, capability, escalation and performance.

For regulators, that can mean the full path from intelligence and risk assessment through education, engagement, monitoring, compliance and enforcement. For departments, it can mean joining policy, service delivery, corporate functions, product, technology and program delivery around one operating model.

Typical work: target operating models, service and process design, governance, organisational interfaces, decision rights, capability models, assurance and implementation roadmaps.

03

RegTech & Technology Design

Design the work before buying the system.

Technology cannot fix an unclear regulatory model or a broken process. We define the operating requirements first, then translate them into technology, data and integration requirements.

We can work with policy and regulatory teams, CIOs, architects, product teams and vendors to define workflows, information, decision support, case management, data, reporting, controls, integration and non-functional requirements. We can also act as the informed client when government needs independent challenge of a vendor or proposed solution.

Typical work: RegTech design, technology strategy, current and target state, requirements, architecture direction, market and option assessment, procurement input, vendor challenge and implementation assurance.

04

Portfolio, Program & Recovery

Spend on the work that matters. Stop spending where it does not.

We help executives make portfolio and program decisions using outcomes, evidence, risk, capacity, dependencies, affordability and benefits—not project momentum.

For work already in trouble, we test the original problem, scope, governance, requirements, commercial model, technology choices and delivery plan. The aim is to find the smallest credible path to the required outcome, not preserve sunk cost.

Typical work: portfolio prioritisation, investment logic, business cases, program assurance, delivery reset, benefits realisation, governance and executive decision support.

05

Implementation & Capability

Leave the organisation able to run it.

We can stay through implementation where that is useful: establishing governance, sequencing work, brokering specialist delivery, supporting adoption and measuring results.

We do not build dependency on advisory support. The operating model, decisions, artefacts and capability are designed to transfer into the organisation.

Typical work: implementation planning, transformation governance, change and adoption, delivery leadership, benefits tracking and capability transfer.
