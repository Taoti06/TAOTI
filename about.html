About

Senior advice for organisations with consequential problems to solve.

Purpose & Practice is a Tasmania- and Victoria-focused advisory practice working across strategy, regulation, operating models, technology, data and transformation.

Why the practice exists

Complex transformations often become programs or procurements before the organisation has established the outcome, operating model, accountabilities and evidence required to make good decisions.

We work in that gap. We help leaders define the problem, determine what capability is required, design how the organisation should operate, align technology and data to that model, establish investment and governance, and remain engaged through implementation and adoption.

How we work

Senior-led and hands-on. The people advising executives do the work.

Outcomes, not activity. Measures begin with the change the organisation is trying to produce.

Technology follows purpose. Platforms and data enable the operating model; they do not define it.

Capability stays with the client. Handover and internal ownership are designed from the beginning.

Experience across government, regulation, essential services and transformation.

The practice draws on senior executive experience spanning enterprise strategy, regulatory design, customer and digital services, portfolio and investment governance, operating-model reform, technology and data, benefits realisation, change and delivery.
