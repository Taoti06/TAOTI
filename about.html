About

Enterprise strategy grounded in how organisations actually work.

Intent & Practice works with government, regulators and complex organisations when the problem crosses functional boundaries and strategy has to become a coherent set of choices, investments and actions.

Integration is the work.

Organisations divide work for good reasons. Strategy, policy, customer, product, operations, finance, people, technology, data and delivery each require specialist capability. But enterprise outcomes fail when those disciplines make decisions independently.

Our role is to connect them: establish the outcome, bring the evidence together, expose dependencies and constraints, frame genuine choices and create clear accountability for execution and benefits.

From direction to delivery.

We work across enterprise strategy, portfolio and investment, operating models, customer and product, technology and data, governance, transformation and benefits realisation.

The work can begin with an enterprise problem, an investment decision, a service or product that needs to change, a regulatory reform, a technology modernisation or a program that has lost its line of sight to the outcome.

Experience

The practice is informed by executive leadership across Victorian Government, Service Victoria and DGS, regulated essential services, emergency management and regulatory environments, together with current advisory work for government and regulators.

That includes enterprise strategy and governance across more than 260 initiatives, portfolio investment exceeding $100 million, DTF-aligned benefits realisation, operating-model reform, Board and ELT strategy and performance, customer and product environments, and deep technology and data leadership.

Technology is part of the enterprise.

Technology, data and digital products are often central to organisational change, but they are not separate from strategy, customer outcomes, operating models or investment.

Deep technology literacy allows us to work credibly with architects, engineers, product, cyber and data specialists while keeping decisions anchored in enterprise outcomes, affordability, risk and value.

The proposition is simple.

See the whole enterprise. Make the choices explicit. Connect the functions required to deliver them. Measure whether the intended value is realised.
