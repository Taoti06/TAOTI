Method

The Regulatory Design Chain.

The method follows the logic of regulation from the problem being addressed to the evidence of regulatory effect. It keeps operating-model, information and technology decisions tied to regulatory purpose.

01

Harm & Risk

What harm or risk is the regulatory regime intended to prevent, eliminate or reduce? What outcome is sought?

02

Regulated Parties & Duty Holders

Who is regulated or holds the relevant duty or obligation? What behaviour is required? What affects compliance?

03

Regulatory Response

What combination of guidance, communications, engagement, permissions, monitoring, compliance and enforcement is appropriate to the risk and behaviour?

04

Operating Model

What capabilities, processes, decisions, governance, workforce and partnerships are required to deliver that response?

05

Information

What evidence, intelligence, records and data are required to assess risk, make decisions, act and evaluate effect?

06

Technology

What digital services, workflows, platforms, integrations, analytics and automation are required to support the model?

07

Regulatory Performance

Is behaviour changing? Is compliance improving? Is risk or harm being reduced? What does the evidence show about regulatory effectiveness?

Communication and engagement are regulatory tools.

Guidance, education, disclosure, consultation and direct engagement can build understanding, influence behaviour and support compliance. They can also generate intelligence about regulated parties, emerging risks and the practical effect of regulation.

Understand the risk and behaviour → select the response → communicate and engage → observe → learn → adapt.

Performance closes the chain.

A regulator needs evidence of more than activity. Performance information should show whether regulatory effort is affecting behaviour, compliance, risk and harm.

That evidence informs the next assessment of risk and the next choice of regulatory response. Technology supports the chain by providing the workflow, records, intelligence and data needed to act and learn.
