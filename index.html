What we know

Regulation is an operating system, not a policy document.

A duty or policy intent only works when it can be translated into real decisions and action: who carries the duty, what harm is being prevented, what information is needed, how risk is assessed, which intervention is appropriate, how decisions are made and recorded, and what technology supports the work.

That is where TAOTI works. Across policy, regulatory practice, operating model, service design, data, technology, investment and delivery.

We design the whole chain, not one piece of it.
